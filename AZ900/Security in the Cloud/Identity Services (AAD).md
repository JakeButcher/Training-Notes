## Overview:
	- Authentication
		○ Establishes if the user or service is who they say they are
			§ Identity is challenged and checked through username and password or authentication keys, certs
	- Authorization
		○ Once the user is authenticated, establishes what level of access they have
			§ Read-only, editor, full admin - what resources and permissions
	- AAD is a cloud based identity service, can integrate with On-Prem
	- All applications running in Cloud or On-Prem can share same credentials
	- Can centralize access control to all apps / data with a single pane of glass over identity management
	- SSO (Single Sign-on)
	- User Management
		○ Customize and control how users sign up and sign in; manage guest users and external partners using your apps
	- Conditional Access
	- Device Management
		○ Manage how cloud and on-prem devices access corporate data
	- PIM (Privileged Identity Management)
		○ Manage, control and monitor access within your organization
				
## Azure PIM:
	- Enables you to manage, control and monitor access to resources
	- Provides time-based and approval-based role activation on resources
	- Examples:
		○ Assign time-bound access to resources
		○ Roles activation upon approval
		○ Enforce MFA to activate any role
		○ Get notifications when privileged roles are activated
