## Overview:
	- Advisor is a free service that provides recommendations on high availability, security, performance, and cost
	- Advisor analyzes your deployed services and looks for ways to improve costs
		○ Optimize VM spending by resizing or shutting down underutilized instances
		○ Reduce costs by eliminating un-provisioned ExpressRoute circuits
		○ Buy reserved VM instances to save money over pay-as-you-go
		○ Delete unassociated public IP Addresses to save money
	
	
	- Cost Management is a free tool that can be used to understand cloud spending
		○ Historical information provided to track spending and compare to budgets set
		○ Can configure budgets and reports to analyze cost areas
