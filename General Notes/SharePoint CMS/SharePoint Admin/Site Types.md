Teams Sites:

A Teams Site is designed to help small groups of people. It’s a place where team members can share documents, track tasks, and have discussions all in one central location. Team Sites are typically private, meaning only members who are invited can see and contribute to the site.



Communication Sites:

A Communication Site is intended for sharing information with a larger audience. (Less collaboration, more communication). These sites are usually more public-facing and are designed to be more visually appealing. They’re perfect for sharing news, reports, statuses, and other information with people inside or outside your organization.



Describe - What are the practical differences between them?

Teams Site would be used internally in a company, usually within a department. An example of this could be a Finance department - the team would be able to share objectives and targets specific to their department, or information they may not want to share with other teams. It can also allow the users to share data and discussions on a smaller scale which can help with privacy.

Whereas, a Communications Site would be intended for a larger amount of people - a real life example of this could be a HR or Company Events department. They would be able to spread information throughout the business on a large scale. This could be helpful when trying to send announcements etc. - This does however, come with a lack of privacy and therefore data sharing is riskier and performed to a lesser degree as more users will have access. (also could be a publicly available site)
